# Kotoba content and rights policy

The reading passages, listening dialogues, questions, answer choices, vocabulary hints, and kana prompts bundled in this prototype are original Kotoba practice content. They were written for this app and are not copied from Genki, Minna no Nihongo, Duolingo, Tofugu, or other commercial learning products.

The app may link learners to third-party learning resources, but a link is not a licence to reproduce their lessons, audio, images, or exercises. Tofugu is therefore used as an external reference only.

## Adding material later

Before importing any external material, record its source, licence, attribution wording, and the date it was checked. Only import content when the licence clearly permits the intended reuse.

- Prefer original writing and recordings for the main course.
- Use openly licensed sentence and dictionary data only after checking each source's current licence and attribution requirements.
- Do not copy textbook pages, commercial app exercises, paid course audio, or web pages whose reuse terms are unclear.
- Obtain model releases or use properly licensed assets for photographs, video, voices, and illustrations.

This keeps the offline A1–B1 practice bank usable without an AI API key, subscription, or hidden copyright risk.
